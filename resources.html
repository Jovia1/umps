Before you join
  • Tell the team about your role in music, events, or a creative business.
  • Ask who can join and which identification or supporting documents are required.
  • Confirm current membership fees, share requirements, and savings contributions.
  • Request the membership rules and application process before making a payment.
Start a membership enquiry
Planning your savings
  • Think about what you are saving for and when you may need the money.
  • Discuss a contribution amount and frequency that fit your income.
  • Ask about withdrawal conditions, charges, and how balances are recorded.
  • Request the current terms for any savings product you are considering.
Ask about savings
Preparing a loan enquiry
  • Describe the purpose of the loan and the amount you want to discuss.
  • Prepare a budget for the personal need, business activity, or event.
  • Ask which documents and membership history the team needs to assess an application.
  • Confirm interest, all fees, guarantor or security requirements, and repayment dates.
  • Ask what happens if income is delayed or a payment is missed.
Ask about loans
Membership rules & documents
  • Contact the office for the current membership rules, application forms, and any SACCO constitution or bylaws.
  • Ask the team for written terms for the savings or loan product you are considering.
  • For questions about personal information, request the SACCO’s current privacy information before sharing documents.
