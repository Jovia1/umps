01 /

Savings

Build the habit of setting money aside for future needs and opportunities.

Ask about savings
  • Discuss savings options and contribution arrangements.
  • Ask about access to savings, charges, and any applicable returns.
  • Choose an approach that fits your income and commitments.
02 /

Loans

Discuss financing for personal needs, business activity, or music and event projects.

Ask about a loan
  • Explain what you need to finance and your proposed budget.
  • Confirm eligibility, required documents, and any guarantor or security requirements.
  • Ask for the full repayment schedule, interest, and fees before deciding.
03 /

Member support

Get help understanding membership, available services, and the next steps for your enquiry.

Speak to the team
  • Ask about joining, fees, and membership responsibilities.
  • Get guidance on savings and loan enquiries.
  • Contact the team when you need help with your membership.
04 /

Financial growth

Use your membership to plan ahead and explore opportunities for yourself and your business.

Discuss your goals
  • Discuss longer-term savings goals with the team.
  • Ask how member benefits work and whether any conditions apply.
  • Request current information about any dividend arrangements.

The team will confirm current product terms, fees, eligibility, and repayment requirements for your enquiry. Making an enquiry does not guarantee loan approval.
