Venue deposits. Production costs. The quiet weeks between events. Working in music comes with its own financial rhythm.

Our community brings savings, credit, and member support into the same conversation.

A shared purpose

We are a member-focused savings and credit cooperative for music promoters, DJs, event organisers, and creative entrepreneurs in Uganda.

Our purpose is to help members build savings, explore financing, and make informed decisions for their personal and working lives.
